What Shapes Clara's Checkout

Jewellery is bought on trust, on a date, and often as a gift

Four things about Clara's store decide what a checkout has to do.

01

Trust decides the sale

A ₹3,000 necklace or a ₹5,499 set is a considered buy. Customers want proof it is real silver, and a payment option that feels safe. Pay After Delivery and risk-checked COD give that comfort.

02

Occasions have dates

Rakhi, birthdays, anniversaries and festive gifting all come with a deadline. A delivery date shown before she buys answers the question that decides a gift purchase.

03

Marketplaces own the customer, your site should

With Clara on Amazon, Myntra, Nykaa, Tata CLiQ, Flipkart and FNP, your own store is where Clara keeps the customer data and the margin. A faster checkout makes it the better place to buy.

04

Offers are everywhere

Strike-through pricing of 16–44%, a festive code like FESTIVE10, and free shipping all compete for attention. Checkout is where they must be shown clearly and not lost.

EDD on the product page, pincode pre-filled

The customer sees a delivery date on the product page itself. For a gift with a date, that answer comes before she decides to buy.

96%+ EDD accuracy · 12–22% PDP-to-cart lift
02

Pre-verified addresses

A returning customer's address is recognised and pre-filled from Shiprocket's delivered history, so fewer high-value parcels fail on a typo.

95%+ pre-fill · up to 99% Tier 1
03

RTO risk tagging on every COD order

Each COD order is scored before it ships. Risky orders can be confirmed first, moved to prepaid, or held, so jewellery is not sent into a likely return.

Up to 45% RTO reduction
04

NDR tags for customer profiling

Past non-delivery behaviour is carried into the order as a tag, so the checkout knows who needs a confirmation and who can go straight through.

05

Pay After Delivery

A UPI mandate that holds the amount and debits only once delivery is confirmed. For a first-time buyer spending on real silver, it removes the last doubt.

06

Partial COD

A small prepaid amount confirms intent, and the balance is paid at the door. It keeps COD's comfort while filtering casual orders.

07

Quick Pay

A returning customer's preferred payment method is pinned as a single tap, so a repeat gift purchase finishes in seconds.

08

Bank offers, sourced by our team

Card and bank offers appear in checkout as their own block, separate from codes like FESTIVE10, so customers see exactly what applies.

09

AI cart upsell

The cart suggests the piece a buyer is most likely to add, such as a matching ring with a pendant, lifting the basket while she is already committed.

10

Serviceability check in checkout

A pincode the courier cannot serve is flagged at checkout, so Clara does not take an order it cannot deliver.

Part Two — fastrr Connect Engage

After the order, the relationship is where repeat gifting comes from

Jewellery is bought again for the next birthday, the next festival and the next gift. fastrr Connect Engage is how Clara reaches those customers, on WhatsApp, SMS, email and RCS, from one place.

Channel

WhatsApp

Festive offers go out as marketing messages. Order confirmations, shipping updates and delivery alerts go out as utility messages. The channel with the highest attention.

Channel

SMS

Reach that does not depend on data or an app. Good for delivery alerts and time-bound sale reminders.

Channel

Email

Room for a story: a gift guide, a gemstone explainer, or a care routine, at a very low cost per send.

Channel

RCS

Rich cards with images and buttons, so a new silver collection shows up as a browsable catalogue inside the messaging app.
